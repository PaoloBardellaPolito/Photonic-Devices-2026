# Fresnel reflection

This folder contains a Python/Colab notebook exploring reflection and transmission at a planar dielectric interface.

## Topics

The notebook covers:

- Snell's law;
- TE and TM Fresnel reflection coefficients;
- reflectivity as a function of incidence angle;
- Brewster angle;
- critical angle and total internal reflection;
- phase of the reflection coefficient in the TIR regime.

### `Fresnel_TE_TM.ipynb`
